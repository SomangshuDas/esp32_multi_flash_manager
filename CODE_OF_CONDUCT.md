# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want ESP32 Multi Flash Manager to be a welcoming project for anyone —
hobbyists, students, and production engineers alike — regardless of
experience level, background, or how they found their way to embedded
development. As a contributor or maintainer, we pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
no matter their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experience
  levels — including disagreements about architecture or design choices.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code, issues,
  and pull requests.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users, not just for
  any one contributor.
- Showing empathy towards other community members, especially newcomers
  asking basic questions about serial flashing, esptool, or Qt.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual
  attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (including hardware serial
  numbers tied to identifiable individuals) without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other official communication channel for
this project — and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private
report through GitHub (Report content / Report abuse on the relevant
issue or PR) or by contacting the maintainer directly through the profile
linked at [github.com/SomangshuDas](https://github.com/SomangshuDas). All
reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to anything they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful — up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces for behavior the maintainers consider harmful to the
community.
